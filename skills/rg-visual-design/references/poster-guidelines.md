# RG Poster & Campaign Visual Guidelines

**Status:** Initial task guide. Apply global requirements from ../brand-principles.md.

## Brief requirements
Confirm:
- Campaign purpose and audience.
- Required headline, supporting copy, CTA, dates, venue, and legal text.
- Required logo and partner assets.
- Canvas size, aspect ratio, channel, and safe area.
- Whether the output is a single poster or a family of related assets.

## Composition
- Establish one primary focal point.
- Give the headline and key message clear priority.
- Group supporting information into readable blocks.
- Keep CTA placement and styling consistent across a campaign.
- Use imagery that supports the message rather than competing with it.
- Preserve logo integrity and clear space using official source assets.
- Keep essential content inside the safe area.

## Art direction
- Define the visual style before generating: photographic, illustrative, geometric, or mixed with explicit rules.
- Standardize lighting, perspective, materials, iconography, and rendering.
- Use RG brand colors and graphic elements only in accordance with the CI.
- Do not add unrelated 3D decorations or effects to fill empty space.
- For a series, approve a master poster or layout before generating variants.

## Exact assets
- Place official logos from original files.
- Insert QR codes from the supplied original asset; never redraw or regenerate them.
- Set exact copy in an editable text layer after image generation.
- Verify CTA destination and QR scan behavior independently.

## Deliverables and QA
Provide the requested raster output and editable source. Verify all copy, dates, venue details, logo, QR code, dimensions, contrast, safe areas, and export integrity.
